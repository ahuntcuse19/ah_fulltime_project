# Demo script (about ten minutes)

Every beat is keyed to the seed. Press **Reset to seed** between runs. Numbers in brackets are the open question to raise at that beat.

**1. Console (30s).** Two rows. Acme: simple, 6 of 16 outstanding, blocked on one person, 3 days open. Northwind: complex, 15 of 52 outstanding, blocked on three named people, 21 days open. Say: "Same product, same form today. The difference is not the documents, it is how many people have to answer." The metrics strip shows n/a for time to first transaction; we come back to it. [8]

**2. Northwind case detail (2 min).** Open it. Left: eight parcels, one per person, plus one marked *Re-request · Bank partner*. Centre: items grouped by subject; every organisation item shows who holds it. Right: timeline; the bank partner's entry is the one with the accent bar. Scroll to the Singapore entity. Read the note on *Articles of incorporation* aloud: paper original from 2004. Say: "This one no software fixes. It is in the demo because a demo where everything resolves is a lie." [17]

**3. Mei Lin Tan's parcel (1 min).** Click *Open as Mei*. She sees four items about herself and one section headed "One more thing from our banking partner" with the reason word for word. She does not see the forty-odd items about a company she does not run. Point at the sensitive-item line under her passport. [1, 12]

**4. Raise a re-request (2 min).** Back to Operator, open Northwind, *Raise re-request*. Bank partner; reason "Certified good standing for the Brazil entity"; *Add row*, Certificate of good standing, Northwind Digital Brasil; send to Rafael Costa. The new parcel appears, the accepted count does not move, the timeline shows origin and reason. Say: "The second ask is where people drop. It is a first-class object here, with a name on it." [4, 5, 14]

**5. Add a document holder (2 min, v2).** Triage, *Prefill as Northwind*, create. *View as* the new Dana Whitfield. Top of her page: people and entities. *Add a person*: an accountant in Mexico, Document holder, holds documents for the Mexico entity. The preview says "Moves 5 items out of Dana Whitfield's list" before she commits. Say: "The admin is choosing the fan-out. The form does not assume she knows everything." [7, 19, 20]

**6. Finish Acme (1 min).** *View as* Jordan Reyes. Five pre-filled values with Confirm buttons, not empty inputs. *Confirm all pre-filled & submit*. Operator: open Acme, *Accept all in review (6)*, *Approve*, *Fund*. Console: median days to first transaction flips from n/a to 3. Say: "Ben's three-to-four-day case. The metric is the one you already chase." [3, 8]

**7. Close (1 min).** Open OPEN_QUESTIONS.md. Lead with question one. Everything tagged "assumed" in the operator view is up for deletion.
